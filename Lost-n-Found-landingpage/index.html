The origins

A shared world.
Good company.

The Lost & Found began as a way for a group of friends to stay connected during a global crisis. Together, we created AVORIA, a D&D campaign that gave us a shared world, shared stories, and something to look forward to each week.

Some of us have taken that same energy beyond the table, making videos, games, art, and other projects together and with our community. We want to share what we create, bring a little joy, and entertain along the way.

Come join us. Our story is better with you in it.
