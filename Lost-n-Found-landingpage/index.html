The origins

A shared world.
Good company.

The Lost & Found began with a group of friends looking to stay conected during a global crisis. One day AVORIA was created. A D&D Campaign built together. We created a shared world, shared stories, and a reason to look forward to next week.

Some of us ventured to bring that same energy beyond the table into videos, games, art, and other projects we make together or with our community. We strive to share our creation, bring some joy, and entertainmnet to the world.

Come Join Us. Our Story is better with you in it.
